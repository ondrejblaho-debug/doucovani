# Angličtina – čtení a poslech (vlastní texty)

@passage en-r1 E-mail from a new friend
Hi Tereza,

My name is Molly and I'm your new e-mail friend from Leeds, a city in the north of England. I'm twelve and I've got a younger brother, Sam. He's eight and he loves dinosaurs.

I go to Hillside School. It's a big school with nine hundred students. My favourite subjects are art and science, but I don't like maths very much. School starts at a quarter to nine and finishes at half past three. After school I usually go swimming on Mondays and Wednesdays. On Saturdays I help my aunt in her small café.

Last weekend we visited my grandparents in Scotland. We went by train and it took five hours! It rained all the time, but we had a great time.

What about you? Have you got any brothers or sisters? Write soon!

Molly
@end

@passage en-r2 Lost and found
FOUND IN CITY PARK – Sunday 14th March

A small brown dog with a red collar. He is very friendly and he likes children. He hasn't got a name tag, but he has got a white spot on his left ear. We are looking after him at the moment.

If he is your dog, please call Martin on 0771 465 230 (after 5 p.m.) or come to 12 Green Street. Please bring a photo of your dog.

No reward needed – we just want him to go home!
@end

@passage en-r3 A day at the science museum
Last Friday our class didn't have normal lessons. Instead, we went to the Science Museum. We met at school at eight o'clock and travelled there by bus. The journey was short, only forty minutes.

First, we saw a show about electricity. A man made his hair stand up with a big metal ball – everybody laughed. Then we went to the space room. You can sit in a real rocket there, but only four people at a time, so we waited for twenty minutes. My friend Jakub was a bit scared, but I loved it.

We had lunch in the museum café. The food was expensive, so most of us ate our own sandwiches outside in the garden. In the afternoon we made simple robots in a workshop. Mine didn't work at first, but the teacher helped me.

We came back to school at four. I was tired but happy. Next year I want to go there again with my parents.
@end

@passage en-r4 Village or city?
Ruby (13): I live in a small village. There isn't a cinema or a shopping centre, but there are fields and a river where I go fishing with my dad. The worst thing is the bus: there are only three buses a day, so I often ask my mum to drive me to my friends. It's very quiet here and I like that.

Leo (14): I live in the centre of a big city. I can walk to school in ten minutes, and there are lots of things to do – I go to a climbing centre twice a week. But the city is noisy and there aren't many green places. In the future I'd like to live near the sea.

Hana (12): We moved from a city to a village last year. At first I was sad because I missed my friends, but now I've got a horse! I still visit the city every month to see my grandma.
@end

@end

@listen en-l3 Poslech 3: In a shop

Assistant: What about these pencils? There are twenty-four colours.
Boy: How much are they?
Assistant: Eight pounds fifty.
Boy: Hmm, that's a bit expensive. Have you got anything cheaper?
Assistant: This sketchbook is four pounds.
Boy: Great, I'll take it. And a card, please.
@end
